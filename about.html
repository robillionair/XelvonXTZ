Neuromorphic AI / Systems Research

DrosophiLLM: Connectome-Constrained Language Model Routing

Spliced an adult Drosophila melanogaster whole-brain connectome (FlyWire Nature 2024; 2,048 neurons, 59,963 synapses) into Layer 12 of Qwen2.5-0.5B using real sparse CSR PyTorch operations (\(O(E)\) complexity) and Dale's Law enforcement:

  • Sparse CSR Kernels with \(O(E)\) Efficiency: Implemented hardware-aligned Compressed Sparse Row (CSR) sparse matrix operations in PyTorch, replacing quadratic dense parameter matrices with direct biological synaptic wiring.
  • Dale's Law Enforcement: Strictly partitioned neurons into non-negative excitatory and non-positive inhibitory neurotransmitter projections, eliminating artificial sign-flipping and aligning artificial routing with biological biophysics.
  • Subcircuit Neuropil Mapping: Grounded linguistic latent states into sensory (antennal lobe, optic lobe), central integrative (central complex, mushroom body), and motor output subcircuits.
