ROBILLIONAIR · DATA PRACTICES

Clarity includes your data.

This page explains what the ANANSI Memory Compiler, ANANSI early-access list and authorized Xelvon company service collect, where information is stored, and how outside AI providers may process company conversations.

EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 10, 2026

What we collect

  • Email address and consent timestamp when you unlock a Memory Compiler download or request ANANSI early access.
  • Conversation content when an authorized Xelvon user submits prompts and receives model responses.
  • Request information, including timestamp, user agent, and IP address for the separate early-access and company-access forms.
  • Mode selection when an authorized Xelvon user chooses Thinking, Fast, or Pro.

Memory Compiler files

Markdown and text files selected in the ANANSI Memory Compiler are read and combined locally inside your browser. Their content and filenames are not uploaded to Robillionair. Only the email and consent data entered at the download step are sent to the server.

How it is used

We use Memory Compiler and ANANSI signup information for occasional product updates and controlled-release invitations. Authorized Xelvon information is used to provide company access, preserve conversation continuity, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

We do not currently use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Where it is stored

Email registration details and conversation history are stored in Google Firebase / Firestore. Your browser also keeps your Xelvon email in session storage for the current browser session.

AI processing

Authorized Xelvon messages are routed through OpenRouter to the model selected in the interface. Those providers process conversation content to generate a response and may apply their own data-handling terms.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, health records, or other highly sensitive information unless your organization has explicitly approved that use.

Retention & deletion

Memory Compiler and early-access records are retained for product communication until they are no longer needed or deletion is requested. Authorized Xelvon records are retained to reconnect company conversations unless deletion is requested or the service no longer needs them. You may request deletion by contacting us.

Security & development status

We use reasonable technical controls, but no internet service can promise absolute security. ANANSI is in development and Xelvon is offered for controlled company use; infrastructure, providers, and features may evolve.

Changes

If these practices materially change, this page and its effective date will be updated. Continued use after an update means the revised practices apply.

Questions, unsubscribe or deletion request?

Contact hello@robillionair.com and include the email used for the relevant ANANSI or Xelvon service.
