02 / The operating philosophy

Memory is sacred infrastructure.

A local-first product creation and intelligence workspace. Windows desktop, built with Tauri 2, and a paired browser interface. Designed around four enduring principles.

I / Integrity

Context with intention.

Compile relevant knowledge into bounded context. Inspect the evidence behind an answer instead of trusting a disappearing chat.

II / Sovereignty

Ownership by default.

Local storage and explicit privacy boundaries. Your workspace remains yours. This pilot stores workspaces and backups in plaintext; encryption and independent certification are not included.

III / Interoperability

Many minds.
One memory.

Work with local models and supported providers on your terms. Review what context reaches the model before you authorize sharing.

IV / Evidence

A record that
can be questioned.

Keep sources, decisions, and artifact lineage inspectable. Synthetic feedback is a tool for exploration, never proof of real demand.
